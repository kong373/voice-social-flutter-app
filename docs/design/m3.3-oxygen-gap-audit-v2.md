# M3.3 Oxygen Gap Audit v2

## Scope

This audit uses the extracted Oxygen video frames under
`/Users/kongzheng/Documents/ny/artifacts/m3-3/video-reference-cc7361ff/`, the
current golden set, and the frozen 69-page product scope from
`lib/app/page_manifest.dart`.

Frozen baseline pages:

- `DS-001` Home
- `DS-004` Discovery feed
- `DS-005` Dynamic detail and comments
- `DS-006` Publish dynamic

Non-goals that must stay out of the product:

- membership or VIP/rights surfaces
- gift backpack or gift inventory surfaces
- third-party flows outside the approved first-party scope

## Severity Summary

| Severity | Status | What it means in this pass |

## Area-Level Audit

### P1: Room, in-room sheets, and room messaging surfaces

Pages:

- `RM-004` to `RM-014`
- `MS-001` to `MS-006`
- `CM-009` to `CM-012`

Why they are P1:

- The source video is dominated by the room’s dark immersive stage, compact header, eight-seat grid, floating message feed, and dense half-sheet tooling.
- The current product still tends to flatten those surfaces into generic cards, generic Material chrome, or tall panels that feel too app-like.
- The gift sheet, more-tools sheet, room management, PK setup, and room battle flows are visually coupled to the room hierarchy, so drift here is immediately obvious.
- Message hub and notification surfaces share the same visual problem: they need the Oxygen light-sky card rhythm and shortcut structure, not a generic inbox shell.

### P2: Account, discovery leftovers, profile, wallet, and community pages

Pages:

- `AC-001` to `AC-012`
- `DS-002`, `DS-003`, `DS-007`, `DS-008`
- `US-001` to `US-010`
- `CM-001` to `CM-008`
- `SC-001` to `SC-007`

Why they are P2:

- These pages are already within the right product family, but the layout rhythm, spacing, and hierarchy still feel too generic compared with the source.
- Account and profile pages need the light sky surface, compact metrics, and denser tool grouping from the video instead of broad empty cards.
- Commerce pages need the source’s split-sheet hierarchy and compact rows; they should not drift into full-screen settings language.
- Community pages need to keep the social/profile language, but the cards, banners, and member management surfaces still need stronger Oxygen density and stronger visual grouping.
- Discovery search, ranking, and saved-room surfaces should sit closer to the accepted home and dynamic language, not become isolated standard forms.

### P3: Polish only

Pages:

- All pages, especially the accepted home and dynamic surfaces, still need small polish checks.

Examples:

- chip radius and pill height consistency
- icon stroke weight and avatar edge softness
- card shadow softness
- type weight balance on dense secondary labels
- sheet transition speed and overlay opacity
- copy trimming where a label is too long for the video rhythm

## Must-Freeze Items

- Keep `DS-001`, `DS-004`, `DS-005`, and `DS-006` as accepted baselines.
- Keep `SC-002` member management in scope. The community surface keeps member management, but it must still follow the Oxygen visual system.
- Do not add membership, VIP rights, gift backpack, or gift inventory surfaces.

## Build Direction

The next design pass should do three things only:

1. Rebuild the room family around the Oxygen immersive stage, eight-seat layout, and half-sheet tooling.
2. Recast the non-room families with the light sky surface, tighter hierarchy, and denser card rhythm.
3. Preserve the frozen home and dynamic pages exactly as accepted.
