# M3.3 Oxygen Page Blueprints v2

This document is the page-level design contract for the 69-page scope in
`lib/app/page_manifest.dart`.

Source grounding:

- extracted Oxygen frames in `/Users/kongzheng/Documents/ny/artifacts/m3-3/video-reference-cc7361ff/`
- the current accepted home and dynamic goldens
- the room, message, commerce, and profile comparison boards already collected in `artifacts/m3-3/design-qa/`

Hard rules:

- keep `DS-001`, `DS-004`, `DS-005`, and `DS-006` frozen as accepted baselines
- keep `SC-002` member management in scope
- do not add membership/VIP rights
- do not add gift backpack or gift inventory
- do not invent new routes outside the approved 69 pages

## Motherboard Dictionary

- `Frozen accepted`: keep the current accepted implementation unchanged.
- `SocialSky Root`: full-page light shell with sky background, white cards, and compact top chrome.
- `SocialSky Form`: light form page with dense inputs, validation, and bottom actions.
- `SocialSky List`: light list or feed page with compact cards and row actions.
- `SocialSky Detail`: light detail page with a primary card, secondary actions, and a bottom composer or footer.
- `SocialSky Sheet`: light half-sheet or modal layered over the social shell.
- `SocialSky Gate`: light fail-closed page for blocked or unavailable states.
- `SocialSky Hub`: light profile or service hub with hero, metrics, and grouped tools.
- `SocialSky Settings`: light settings-style surface with grouped toggles and rows.
- `RoomImmersive Stage`: dark room main surface with the eight-seat layout and floating content.
- `RoomImmersive Sheet`: dark half-sheet or modal layered over the room stage.
- `RoomImmersive Tools`: dark tool surface for room actions, management, and controls.
- `RoomImmersive Overlay`: dark reconnect or warning overlay that never leaves the room context.
- `RoomImmersive Battle`: dark PK battle and settlement surface.
- `Commerce Card`: compact wallet or order card surface.
- `Commerce Sheet`: commerce half-sheet or form surface.
- `Commerce Detail`: commerce detail or timeline surface.
- `Commerce Result`: commerce result or return surface.
- `Community Hub`: light community hub with hero and grouped cards.
- `Community List`: light list or management surface for community relations.
- `Community Detail`: light community detail surface with a focused card and supporting actions.
- `Community Sheet`: light or mixed community modal surface.
